WhataboutageOutage Router: nuclear outage risk tracking

An outage-planning agent that warns planners about risks that came up in past planned or forced outages before they happen again. Upload a schedule as PDF, Excel or CSV and it flags the tasks most likely to go wrong, explaining why with cited lessons learned and recommended mitigations.

MongoDB Atlas is the agent's governed memory. Each lesson is a 384-dimension embedding matched by Atlas Vector Search, and $graphLookup follows cause-and-effect links between failures. Contractors get redacted, read-only views enforced inside the Atlas query, corrections are versioned, forgotten lessons expire through a TTL index, and every change lands in an audit log.
